## ⚡ What is DealShield AI?

Freelancers, agency founders, and independent consultants routinely sign client agreements without legal review because attorneys charge $300 to $600 per hour.

**DealShield AI** is an autonomous contract risk intelligence studio that instantly redlines agreements and protects service providers against catastrophic legal exposure:

1. **Contract Health Score (0–100):** Real-time severity grading (A, B, C, D, F) with risk breakdown.
2. **5 Core Risk Analyzers:**
   - 🔴 **Unlimited Liability & Consequential Damages:** Catches uncapped damages and indemnification traps.
   - 🔴 **IP & Tool Forfeiture:** Protects your pre-existing code libraries, boilerplates, and frameworks from client grabs.
   - 🟡 **Payment Delays & Pay-When-Paid:** Detects Net-60/90 stalling and lack of late payment interest.
   - 🟡 **Scope Creep & Revisions:** Eliminates subjective "complete satisfaction" traps and bounds revision rounds.
   - 🟡 **Restrictive Covenants:** Flags ruinous global non-competes and excessive non-solicitation clauses.
3. **The Trap & The Fix (Attorney-Vetted Replacement Clauses):** Every flagged clause includes a plain-English explanation of the danger and a 1-click copy-paste counter-clause.
4. **Client Negotiation Email Generator:** Auto-generates a firm, professional, courteous email to the client detailing the required redlines.
5. **Executive PDF Audit Report:** 1-click download of a branded, comprehensive PDF Redline Assessment Report with zero server processing.

## 🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Legal documents and client NDAs contain confidential trade secrets, pricing, and project roadmaps. 
* **Zero Cloud Document Storage:** DealShield AI runs 100% in the user's browser using client-side JavaScript.
* **No Database:** Contract text is never transmitted or stored on any remote database or server.
* **Offline Capable:** Full rule engine functions even without an active internet connection.
